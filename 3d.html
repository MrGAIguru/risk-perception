How it works here

01You have your own legal duty

Every worker must take reasonable care of themselves and anyone their work affects, and co-operate with the employer.

02Any height counts

Work at height is anywhere you could fall far enough to get hurt. There is no safe minimum height.

03Avoid · prevent · minimise

Avoid working at height if you can. If not, prevent the fall. If a fall is still possible, minimise how far and how badly.

04Every roof is fragile

Treat every roof as fragile until a competent person confirms it is not. Almost one in five construction deaths involve roof work.

05Induction before you start

The principal contractor must give you a suitable site induction before you start work.

06Speak up, you’re protected

Report risks to whoever controls the work. The law protects you for raising a genuine safety concern.
